abstract: MACLAS dataset  has been constructed by coupling the elemental and mineral
  information of (n =1243 samples) from 52 sites in Portugal and Spain in a chronological
  span from 5th to 3rd millennium BC. Each data point is described by a vector of
  45 independent variables with the elemental concentrations expressed in atomic percentages
  obtained by p-XRF associated with a  single categorical mineral label obtained by
  XRD as the dependent variable. This resource, result of a project spanning more
  than a decade, has been made possible by the hard work, collaborative spirit and
  open-science vision of many researchers.
